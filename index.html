How does NexaAI protect my data?

Nexa AI employs enterprise-grade AES-256 encryption both in transit and at rest, maintaining full compliance with GDPR and SOC 2 Type II standards. Your corporate data is strictly isolated and is never used to train public machine learning models.

Can I change my subscription plan at any time?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription plan directly from your account dashboard at any time. All plan adjustments take effect immediately, with pro-rated billing applied automatically to your account balance.

Are custom integrations available?

Absolutely. Our platform provides robust REST and GraphQL APIs alongside webhooks, allowing seamless connection with your existing enterprise software. Custom dedicated connectors can also be engineered upon request for enterprise plans.

What onboarding support does NexaAI provide?

We offer comprehensive onboarding support including dedicated technical account managers, live interactive team training, and 24/7 priority customer service. Enterprise clients also receive personalized assistance for initial workflow setup and API integration.
